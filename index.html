Tessera Sketches

One thing was sent. The screen is made for whoever opens it.

It starts as yours: it asks you three things, then makes your home from everything you have. Nobody received a layout. What travels is the idea: statements and numbers, or a helper as what it is for, needs, does and asks. Each device makes its own screen from what it knows about its person. Pick anyone else below to see what they get from the same things.

A working sketch, not a product. What it learns about you, what you make or are sent, and a screen designed for you are kept in this browser and nowhere else; “Forget everything” erases them. Nothing leaves the page unless you copy something to send, ask Claude, keep a copy in your Claude Docs, or save a file for your own AI, and it says what goes first. The three things to read are real containers, read here as they are; the two helpers are ideas with no code. Every screen is made by a composer from what was sent and what the device knows. The sample people’s calendars and clock are stand-ins. Yours are real: this device’s clock, a calendar file you give it (only the hours taken are kept), and whatever way of telling you this browser allows. The Ukrainian wording, the short headlines and the captions under numbers of the first two things stand for what a model writes once and the thing then carries; each is marked, with the author’s own words one tap away.
